Route to **cs-ceo-advisor** — Strategic Advisory workflow.

1. Ask the user to describe: the decision or topic, current context, constraints, and what outcome they need (if not in $ARGUMENTS).
2. Read the CEO advisor skill:
   ```bash
   cat c-level-advisor/ceo-advisor/SKILL.md
   ```
3. Analyse the situation across four dimensions:
   - **Strategic:** Market positioning, competitive landscape, company direction
   - **Financial:** Revenue impact, burn rate, fundraising implications
   - **Organizational:** Team, culture, stakeholder alignment
   - **Risk:** Key risks and mitigation strategies
4. Frame the recommendation for the relevant audience:
   - Board presentation format (if board-related)
   - Investor narrative (if fundraising-related)
   - Executive summary (if internal decision)
5. Deliver: Structured brief with recommendation, supporting rationale, and next steps.
