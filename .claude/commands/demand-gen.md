Route to **cs-demand-gen-specialist** — Demand Generation workflow.

1. Ask the user for: target audience, primary channel (paid search, paid social, content, ABM, etc.), goal (leads, pipeline, brand), and budget (if not in $ARGUMENTS).
2. Read the demand acquisition skill:
   ```bash
   cat marketing-skill/marketing-demand-acquisition/SKILL.md
   ```
3. Build the demand gen strategy:
   - Audience segmentation and ICP definition
   - Channel selection with rationale and expected CPL
   - Funnel stages: Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Intent → Conversion
   - Messaging framework per stage
   - Budget allocation across channels
4. Define KPIs and measurement plan:
   - Leading indicators (CTR, CPC, conversion rate)
   - Lagging indicators (MQL, SQL, pipeline, revenue)
   - Reporting cadence
5. Deliver: Demand gen strategy document ready to execute.
