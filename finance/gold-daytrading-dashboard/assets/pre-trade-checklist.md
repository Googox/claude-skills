# Pre-Trade Checklist

Print it, or keep it open. Every box is answered out loud before the order is
placed. An unanswered box is a no.

## Before the session

- [ ] Economic calendar checked. High-impact releases noted with times.
- [ ] Prior day high and low marked on the chart.
- [ ] Asian session range marked.
- [ ] H4 and H1 bias written down in one sentence each, before the session starts.
- [ ] Account balance and today's risk budget confirmed.
- [ ] Trades taken today: ___ of 2 permitted.
- [ ] Running P&L today: ___ R of the 2R daily loss limit.

## Before the entry

- [ ] The setup is one of the four permitted types: trend pullback, level
      rejection, break and retest, session open continuation.
- [ ] The higher timeframe agrees with the direction, or there is a written
      reason why the counter-trend trade is justified.
- [ ] The entry sits at a marked level, not in the middle of a range.
- [ ] The stop is at the structural invalidation, chosen before the size.
- [ ] The target is a level that exists on the chart.
- [ ] Reward-to-risk to Target 1 is at least 1.5. Value: ___
- [ ] Position size computed with `position_sizer.py`, not estimated. Lots: ___
- [ ] No high-impact release within the next 30 minutes.
- [ ] I would take this trade if the last one had been a loss.
- [ ] I can state the strongest argument against this trade. It is: ___

## After the exit

- [ ] Trade logged with `trade_journal.py add` within ten minutes of the exit.
- [ ] `followed_plan` answered honestly, including when the trade won.
- [ ] Setup type and session recorded.
- [ ] One sentence on what actually happened, not what should have happened.

## Weekly

- [ ] `trade_journal.py stats --by setup` reviewed.
- [ ] `trade_journal.py stats --by session` reviewed.
- [ ] Off-plan trades read individually.
- [ ] Total hours spent this week on trading: ___
- [ ] Total R this week: ___
- [ ] Honest answer: was this week's time better spent here or on client work?

The last question is the one that decides whether this activity survives.
